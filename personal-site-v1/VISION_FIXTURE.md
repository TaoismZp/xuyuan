# Vision Fixture — Personal Site v1 / Frontend

Identity: `vision-personal-site-v1-2026-11-03-a`

## Product truth
This is TaoismZp's personal website. It is not a blog product and not a finance media site. A visitor should first understand a person: a long-term secondary-market trader who writes and researches. Longform essays are the strongest public evidence of that person, but books/projects/about/notes must feel equally native to the architecture.

## First-view promise
The first viewport should feel like opening a private decision notebook that has been deliberately made public — precise, calm, opinionated, not luxurious for luxury's sake and not decorated with finance clichés.

The memorable line is:

> 我把钱放进市场，也把判断写下来。

This line, not a logo animation or decorative object, is the one bold move.

## Visual world
- Cool paper rather than warm cream; deep ink rather than tinted near-black.
- One cobalt signal used sparingly for active/structural emphasis, not gradient decoration.
- Chinese sans carries the personal-site shell; serif is reserved for longform titles/body moments where reading is the artifact.
- Large scale comes from authored language and whitespace, not oversized role labels.
- Structural lines encode grouping and chronology. No rounded-card wallpaper.
- No stock photography, candlesticks, coins, charts, suit-and-tie portraits, glassmorphism, bento SaaS grammar, fake terminal UI, or generic editorial broadsheet cosplay.

## Information architecture visible from home
1. Person / authored thesis
2. Recent longform
3. Long-term projects
4. Short about statement
5. Clear paths to Essays / Projects / About

## Responsive behavior
Mobile is composed independently: the hero becomes one decisive statement followed by a compact identity note; essay rows remain rows, projects stack as full-width fields, and touch targets remain >=44px. No desktop-only visual trick may be necessary to understand the site.

## Article surface
Article pages enter `Read` mode. The shell recedes. Reading width is 680–760px, Chinese body is >=18px mobile / >=19px desktop, generous line-height, real headings and blockquotes, visible publication metadata, share/back controls with restrained styling, and a quiet reading-progress affordance.

## Must-match
- Person-first first viewport.
- One authored statement as the memorable element.
- Longform presented as editorial rows, not cards.
- Project area visually distinct from essay area without becoming a dashboard.
- Cool-paper / ink / cobalt palette.
- Mobile remains authored, not merely collapsed.

## Allowed innovation
Typography scale, exact column proportions, project composition, motion details, and navigation behavior may evolve if they strengthen the product truth.

## Anti-reference
The earlier `longform-v2` and all bakeoff spikes are evidence only. None of their screenshots, markup, or visual motifs are allowed to be reused verbatim as the final product surface.
