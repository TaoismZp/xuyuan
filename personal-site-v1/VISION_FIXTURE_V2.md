# Vision Fixture v2 — User-selected minimalist direction

Status: LOCKED FOR BUILD
User selection: third minimalist concept from 2026-11-03 review

## Product identity
A personal website for TaoismZp. It should feel like a quiet, durable personal publication made by a long-term trader/writer, not a trading dashboard, SaaS landing page, cyberpunk terminal, or generic blog template.

## First-impression target
- warm ivory ground, near-black ink
- oversized Chinese serif hero: “长期交易，长期写作。”
- one restrained olive signal colour only
- no cards on the homepage
- no gradients, glow, ticker, market dashboard, decorative charts, glassmorphism, icon grids, or AI-style ornamental UI
- thin rules, large whitespace, strong typography
- article list behaves like an editorial index
- projects are typographic, not promotional tiles

## Information order
1. identity / thesis
2. latest long-form essays
3. long-term projects
4. navigation to essays, projects, about

## Reading contract
The homepage may be visually bold through scale, but the article surface must become quieter: narrow reading measure, serif Chinese body, generous line height, minimal tools, no decorative chrome.

## Responsive contract
On phone the design stays typographic and spacious; nothing becomes a stack of cards. Navigation remains usable with 44px targets, titles wrap deliberately, and article body text stays >=18px.

## Explicit rejections
Trading-terminal UI, sci-fi dashboard, dense stats, full-bleed imagery, image-card grids, blue accents, bright neon, multi-colour palettes, ornamental badges, rounded SaaS cards.

## Approval gate
This fixture is based on the user explicitly choosing the third minimalist concept. Engineering acceptance does not replace a later user visual review of the real browser build.
