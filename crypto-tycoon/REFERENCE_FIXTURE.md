# Crypto Tycoon — Reference Fixture

## Locked target
The visual target is the generated 16:9 concept image from the 2026-11-02 build session titled **“币圈大富翁霓虹棋盘盛宴”**.

- source dimensions: 1672 × 941
- local source SHA-256 at lock time: `5b0c5d5d4f8f9afbd2af5e8dddbd77e749975006873262efadd02125ac68a039`
- visual intent: polished commercial casual-game screenshot, not a flat webpage/dashboard

## Must-match qualities
1. **World, not tilted UI** — a volumetric board with real spatial depth and authored landmarks.
2. **Hero board silhouette** — a square-loop tycoon board dominates the frame with a luminous central market/title stage.
3. **Authored tile landmarks** — exchange tower, bull, bear, rocket/halving, whale, mining rig, staking/vault, NFT/DeFi motifs.
4. **Character tokens** — four differentiated stylized player pieces, not colored dots or emoji placeholders.
5. **Material hierarchy** — gold, glass/neon, dark metal, colored tile materials, reflections/highlights and contact shadows.
6. **Lighting** — warm gold key light + cyan/violet finance-neon rim lighting; visible depth and separation.
7. **Integrated game HUD** — leaderboard, round/goal, asset/rule actions and a large dice control arranged around the board, not a generic side dashboard.
8. **Density** — central dice/coins/market stage plus skyline/backdrop details; no large empty prototype zones.
9. **Game readability** — despite density, tile categories, player position, current action and market state remain legible.
10. **Mobile/iPad delivery** — the composition must remain recognizably the same game world on a narrow viewport rather than collapsing into a different dashboard.

## Not pixel-copy requirements
- exact crypto logos, character faces, tile wording and building shapes may differ;
- the final game may simplify geometry for mobile performance;
- gameplay content may exceed the concept image.

## Comparative Quality Gate
Before Final, capture the actual game at a representative active-play state on desktop/tablet, compare it beside the locked concept at the same displayed size, and score:
- silhouette / composition
- depth / volume
- authored forms
- materials
- lighting
- character quality
- landmark density
- UI-world cohesion
- readability
- desirability

If the pair still reads as “concept art vs ordinary webpage”, the build is not Final regardless of functional test results.
