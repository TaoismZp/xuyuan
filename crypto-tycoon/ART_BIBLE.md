# Crypto Tycoon — Art Bible v1

## Reference Fixture
Locked visual target: the approved 16:9 neon 3D board-game key art produced before implementation. It is the perceptual benchmark, not a loose inspiration.

## Player Promise
Open the game and immediately feel like a polished commercial crypto board game: toy-like premium 3D pieces, bright casino/arcade spectacle, a readable loop board, memorable crypto landmarks, and a central hero stage.

## Camera / Composition
- Desktop reference: 16:9, three-quarter top-down hero camera, approx. 32–38° pitch.
- Board occupies roughly 72–82% of width and 78–88% of height.
- Center stage is the dominant focal point; board path wraps it tightly.
- Foreground dice/cards/coins create depth; city skyline and financial screens create background scale.
- HUD lives at the left and right edges and must not cover the core board route.
- No large dead gray/black areas.

## Shape Language
- Rounded chunky toy proportions, bevels everywhere, no sharp CAD-like raw boxes.
- Major props have readable silhouettes at thumbnail size.
- Landmarks exaggerate one semantic cue: bull horns, whale body, exchange tower, rocket plume, NFT pixel portrait, mining rigs.
- Characters read as collectible figurines on glowing bases, not primitives with accessories.

## Material Roles
- Gold: polished metallic hero trim, coins, crown, dice pips / rim accents.
- Board tiles: glossy enamel / premium plastic with soft bevel highlights.
- Dark base: blue-black lacquer, not flat gray.
- Hologram / market screens: cyan-magenta emissive glass.
- Characters: matte-to-gloss toy resin with metallic accessory accents.

## Lighting
- Warm gold key from upper-left/front.
- Cyan/blue rim from right/background.
- Violet ambient fill.
- Contact shadows under every figurine, dice and landmark.
- Background practical lights and signs create depth.
- Bloom is secondary polish only; it cannot substitute for modeled detail.

## Color / Contrast
- Saturated but legible palette: gold + cyan + violet + pink + green + red.
- Board center stays darker so gold logo and cyan market globe read clearly.
- Tiles are individually differentiated by hue but share the same material system.
- Text on tiles must remain dark-on-light or light-on-dark with strong contrast.

## Density Layers
1. Foreground: dice, event cards, coins/chips, roll control.
2. Midground: 24 board tiles, 4 character figurines, landmarks.
3. Hero center: logo/crown, market globe / candlestick display.
4. Background: skyline, exchange tower, neon crypto symbols, market charts.

## UI Language
- Left: ranked players with portrait/avatar, cash/net worth, market delta.
- Right top: round / objective.
- Right middle: compact vertical actions.
- Right bottom: oversized physical-looking roll-dice control.
- UI uses gold edging, dark translucent navy panels, high hierarchy, not generic debug cards.

## Non-Negotiable Visual Gates
- Active-play screenshot must not read as a sparse developer scene.
- No primitive hero figures, no empty background, no gray void.
- Center stage must visually dominate and feel authored.
- At least 6 signature 3D landmarks visible in the main camera.
- At least 3 depth layers visible simultaneously.
- Screenshot at equal size must feel like the same product family as the Reference Fixture.
