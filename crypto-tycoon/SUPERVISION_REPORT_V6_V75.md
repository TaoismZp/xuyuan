## Why it still fails
1. **Center monument blocks too much board** — the large vertical sign reads as a billboard placed in front of the game rather than an integrated crown/globe stage. The effect drawing keeps the title heroic while preserving the board loop and playable surfaces.
2. **Character sculpt quality is still prototype-grade** — astronaut / Doge / NFT / bull are readable, but several silhouettes still reveal primitive construction before they read as collectible toys.
3. **City authored-form repetition remains visible** — many towers share the same rectangular body + neon-strip language, so the backdrop reads procedural compared with the more varied financial city in the reference.
4. **Lighting has local clipping** — the left-front character/tile area is overexposed and loses material separation; black values in the background are also compressed.
5. **Tile information is borderline at camera distance** — the front loop is readable, but several upper/right tiles become decorative color slabs before their economic meaning reads.
